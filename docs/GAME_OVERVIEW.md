# Cyclical TCG — Game Overview

# Vision

Cyclical TCG is a competitive online PvP trading card game focused on:

* tactical lane combat
* resource planning
* evolving card value
* low randomness
* fast but meaningful decision making

The game combines:

* unique deck identity inspired by Commander-style deckbuilding
* resource management inspired by modern resource-based TCGs
* constant turn tension inspired by highly interactive combat systems
* positional combat through fixed lane gameplay

The core identity of the game is:

# progression through cycling

Players continuously recycle resources, evolve card value over time, and adapt their board state through multiple game cycles.

---

# Core Gameplay Pillars

# 1. Every Card Has Multiple Purposes

Cards are never just:

* playable units
* spells

Every card may also function as:

* future resource
* future scaling
* Revolve fuel
* Primed setup

Players constantly choose between:

* immediate tempo
* long-term value

---

# 2. Tactical Lane Combat

Combat is:

* deterministic
* positional
* readable

Units attack only:

* the opposing unit in the same lane

If the opposing lane is empty:

* the attack hits the enemy player directly

This creates:

* board tension
* lane management
* positional strategy
* defensive planning

---

# 3. Cyclical Progression

The game uses a unique:

# Revolve system

Infused cards are placed into the:

* Revolve Pile

When the deck becomes empty:

* the Revolve Pile is shuffled into a new deck

This creates:

* escalating power over time
* evolving card interactions
* long-term planning
* cyclical pacing

The game is designed around:

# multiple gameplay cycles

rather than traditional linear mana progression.

---

# 4. Low Randomness, High Agency

The game minimizes:

* random targeting
* excessive RNG
* high-variance effects

Most effects:

* target directly
* provide deterministic outcomes
* reward planning and sequencing

The goal is:

# player decisions determine outcomes more than luck

---

# 5. Fast Competitive Matches

Target match duration:

* ideally under 10 minutes
* maximum around 15 minutes

The game is designed to:

* reduce downtime
* encourage constant engagement
* support online competitive play
* remain readable even during complex turns

---

# Core Mechanics

# Infuse

Players use cards from hand as temporary resources.

Each card contains:

* an Infuse Value

Infused cards are sent to the:

* Revolve Pile

This creates meaningful tradeoffs between:

* playing cards
* investing resources
* preparing future cycles

---

# Revolve

When a player's deck becomes empty:

* shuffle the Revolve Pile into a new deck

This is called:

# Revolve

Many cards become stronger:

* after Revolves
* after being Infused
* after becoming Primed

---

# Primed

Some cards become:

# Primed

after meeting specific conditions, usually involving:

* Infusing
* Revolving
* repeated cycling

Primed cards often gain:

* stronger stats
* upgraded effects
* additional utility

---

# Ace Cards

Players choose:

* one Legendary card as their Ace

The Ace begins outside the deck in the:

* Ace Zone

The Ace becomes playable:

* after the player's First Revolve

Ace cards represent:

* long-term strategy
* class identity
* late-game power spikes

Legendary cards may still:

* be included in the main deck normally

---

# Turn Structure

Each turn follows:

```txt
Start Turn
↓
Main Phase
↓
Attack Phase
↓
End Turn
```

---

# Main Phase

Players may:

* summon units
* cast spells
* activate unit abilities
* Infuse cards for resources

---

# Attack Phase

All units:

* must attack if able
* attack from left to right

Combat rules:

* attack opposing lane only
* simultaneous damage
* direct attack if lane is empty

This creates:

* predictable combat flow
* tactical sequencing
* positional pressure

---

# End Turn System

At the end of the turn:

1. Keep up to 2 cards in hand
2. Remaining cards are sent to the Revolve Pile
3. Draw until holding 5 cards

Players do NOT draw at the start of their turn.

This system:

* reduces downtime
* allows planning during the opponent's turn
* keeps both players mentally engaged

---

# Damage Persistence

Unit damage remains permanently unless healed.

Example:

* a damaged unit stays damaged across turns

This creates:

* lasting combat consequences
* board attrition
* strategic trading

---

# Hidden Information

Public Information:

* battlefield
* discard piles
* Revolve Piles
* Life totals

Hidden Information:

* player hands
* unrevealed deck order

---

# Match End

A player loses when:

* their Life reaches 0

If a player must draw while:

* their deck is empty
* and their Revolve Pile is empty

they take:

* 1 damage per missing draw

If both players lose simultaneously:

* the match ends in a draw

---

# Design Philosophy

Cyclical TCG is designed around:

* meaningful decisions
* positional gameplay
* evolving resource systems
* deterministic interactions
* competitive readability

The game rewards:

* planning ahead
* efficient resource usage
* combat positioning
* long-term cycle management

rather than:

* high randomness
* explosive solitaire turns
* hidden combo chains

---

# Intended Experience

Players should feel:

* constant tactical tension
* meaningful tradeoffs every turn
* gradual escalation of power
* attachment to their Ace strategy
* satisfaction from careful sequencing

The game should feel:

* strategic but fast
* complex but readable
* competitive but expressive

with every turn contributing toward:

# an evolving gameplay cycle
